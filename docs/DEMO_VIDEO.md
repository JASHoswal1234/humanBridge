## Recording and submission checklist

- Use your original requester profile for the settled batch. Verify access before recording. Where prior checkout footage is unavailable, show the stored authorization and read-back evidence and describe it in the past tense.
- Record product screens at a legible size. Hide credentials, email, address bar tokens, reviewer links and unrelated tabs. Use synthetic images and your own voice. Captions should identify Sandbox and any cuts between batches.
- Avoid repeating a charge for footage. Do not present scripted receipts or mocked test screenshots as a live transaction. If the historical dashboard cannot be opened, show the sanitized evidence log with its date and label the source.
- Export below 3:00, add captions, watch it signed out, verify audio and public viewing permissions. Check the repository, setup guide, license and any hosted URL signed out. Enter the links and team details in the submission form. The team owner confirms and submits.
